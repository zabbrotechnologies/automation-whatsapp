COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Technical Questions

Everything you need to know about the Baileys companion protocol and OpenRouter integration.

How does the companion device protocol prevent WhatsApp bans?

Unlike unauthorized modified WhatsApp APKs or unofficial reverse-engineered tools, our system connects using the official Multi-Device Companion protocol (the same technology WhatsApp Web uses). Messages are transmitted over standard secure WebSockets, and our anti-ban jitter engine inserts randomized pauses (2.5–5.0 seconds) to mirror natural human behavior.

Do I need to install or run Google Apps Script in my spreadsheet?

No! That is the core innovation of Flow. You simply set your Google Sheet share setting to "Anyone with the link can view" and paste the link into our dashboard. The engine fetches the public CSV stream directly, extracts the recipient list, and starts dispatching immediately.

How does the trigger-word filter ensure personal messages are ignored?

The server intercepts incoming messages and passes them through a keyword gatekeeper. Only if the message contains explicit buying intent words (such as price, cost, catalog, buy, or demo) does the AI activate. Friends, family, and group chats (@g.us) are completely ignored.

Are the OpenRouter AI models really 100% free?

Yes! OpenRouter provides generous free tiers for high-performance open-source models like Google Gemma 2 9B (`google/gemma-2-9b-it:free`) and NVIDIA Nemotron 70B (`nvidia/llama-3.1-nemotron-70b-instruct:free`). You do not need to add a credit card or pay per-token fees.
